About Us

Our Journey & Leadership

From a small coaching initiative to a growing educational mission.

Our History

2021: Our journey began as a small coaching centre in Bathangachhi village.

2025: We started residential coaching under the name Rise English Academy in Bhurulia, serving Classes I–VIII.

2026: The initiative developed into M.M. English Medium Mission, with an educational vision for Classes I–X.

The two “M” letters represent MAHIBUL SEKH and MUKTAR SEKH, whose vision inspires the M.M. initiative.
